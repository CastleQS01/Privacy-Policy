Privacy Policy for Arrow ClearPath

Effective date: 1 November 2026 · Last updated: 1 November 2026

Karuna Satkar ("we", "us", "our") provides the mobile game Arrow ClearPath (the "App"). This policy explains what information is handled when you download, install or play the App, and the choices you have. Arrow ClearPath is intended for players aged 13 and over. Our contact details are in Section 9. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

Summary

  • No account. You never give us your name, email, phone number or password to play.
  • Game progress, settings, coins and purchases are stored only on your device.
  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob, which may collect your advertising ID, device details, IP‑based approximate location and ad interactions.
  • Personalized ads require your consent (opt‑in) in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, have an opt‑out in the US, and are on by default elsewhere — with a control in Settings → Privacy → Ad preferences.
  • In‑app purchases are processed by Google Play; we never see your card or billing details.
  • Analytics and crash reporting use Google Firebase (Analytics + Crashlytics), consent‑gated where required.
  • We do not sell your personal information for money.

1. Information we handle

We request no sensitive permissions: the App does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

1.1 Stored only on your device

Your level progress, settings, coins, stars, ad‑consent choice and purchase entitlements are saved locally using standard app storage. This information is not transmitted to us and is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

1.2 Advertising data (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads through Google AdMob (Google LLC, and Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA/UK/Switzerland). To deliver, cap, measure and protect ads against fraud, Google and its ad partners may automatically collect your device's advertising identifier, device information (model, OS, language, screen size), your IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it, ad‑interaction data, and ad diagnostics. Google processes this as an independent controller under its own privacy policy. We receive only aggregated reports (impressions, earnings).

1.3 Purchase data (Google Play Billing)

If you buy an item such as "Remove Ads" or premium content, Google Play processes the payment. We receive a purchase token and order confirmation so the App can deliver and restore your purchase. We do not receive or store your name, payment card or billing address.

1.4 Analytics and crash reporting (Google Firebase)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the game is played and to find and fix crashes. These collect random installation identifiers, device model and OS, App version, language, approximate (country‑level) location derived from IP, gameplay events (e.g. levels started/completed, purchases), and crash logs. Google acts as our data processor. We use this data in aggregate to improve and stabilize the game — not to identify you and not to personalize ads.

1.5 Support email

If you email us for help, we receive your email address and whatever you include, used only to respond.

3. Advertising and your choices

Ads keep Arrow ClearPath free. You control how they use your data:

  • EEA / UK / Switzerland: before personalized ads are requested, the App shows a consent message (Google's User Messaging Platform). You can accept or decline. The same choice governs whether analytics (1.4) runs there.
  • United States: personalized ads are on by default; you can opt out at Settings → Privacy → Ad preferences ("Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information"). We then request restricted‑data‑processing / non‑personalized ads.
  • Elsewhere: personalized ads are on by default; you can still turn them off at Settings → Privacy → Ad preferences.
  • Your device: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Ad content rating: ads are limited to content rated suitable for teens (maximum rating "T") for every user.
  • Remove Ads: if you purchase Remove Ads, the App stops requesting all ads (banner, interstitial and rewarded); hint and undo helpers become unlimited, so rewarded ads are no longer offered.

If you decline personalized ads you will still see ads, but based on general context rather than your interests. Google may still use limited data for frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention.

4. Age, teens and children

  • Minimum age. You must be at least 13 (or the higher minimum digital‑consent age where you live).
  • Under 13. The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
  • No age verification. The App does not ask your age or date of birth; we rely on the App's Google Play age rating and the minimum age above.
  • Teen safeguards (all players): ad content is capped at a Teen rating; personalized ads require consent in the EEA/UK/CH and can be turned off everywhere at Settings → Privacy → Ad preferences; and we do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of players under 18 for targeted advertising.
  • Parents/guardians. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us (Section 9) and we will stop that processing and delete any such data we hold.

5. Who receives information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. It is disclosed only to:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited) — advertising; independent controller. policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase (Analytics + Crashlytics) — analytics/crash reporting; our processor. firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play (Google LLC / Google Commerce Limited) — purchases; independent controller. policies.google.com/privacy
  • [Email provider] — support email.

We may also disclose information if required by law or a valid authority request; to protect rights, safety or property; or to a successor if the App is sold (this policy then continues to apply). International transfers: Google may process data in the US and other countries; where EEA/UK/CH data is transferred, Google relies on safeguards such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (with UK/Swiss extensions) and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.
